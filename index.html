Our Story

About Panchgaon

"Arrive. Indulge. Rediscover."

Panchgaon Restaurant is perched in the foothills of the Aravali Ranges — one of the most unique areas, named after a cluster of five villages in District Manesar.

We welcome each guest with open arms to experience a truly unique highway dining experience. High-quality food, efficient and friendly service, luxurious facilities, magical ambience, and very affordable prices — all while being deeply rooted in true Indian ethos and age-old desi traditions.

The fine balance between contemporary settings and old traditions will dazzle you, as you go on a journey to discover Panchgaon — and in turn discover your own "desi"-ness.
